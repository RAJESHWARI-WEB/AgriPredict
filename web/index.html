Know what to grow and what to feed it, before you plant a single seed.

AgriPredict takes your soil's N-P-K values, pH, and local climate readings, then uses two trained classification models to suggest the crop most likely to thrive and the fertilizer that will actually help it.

Two tools, one dataset-backed pipeline

  • 🌾 Crop Recommendation

    Enter your soil's N-P-K, pH, temperature, humidity and rainfall. A Random Forest model trained on 2,200+ field samples across 22 crops returns the best match plus two runner-ups.

  • 🧪 Fertilizer Recommendation

    Pick your soil type and the crop you're growing, and the model cross-checks your N-P-K levels against what that crop actually needs to suggest the right fertilizer mix.

  • 📊 Confidence, not just a label

    Every result comes with a confidence score and the runner-up predictions, so you can see how clear-cut the recommendation really is instead of trusting a black box.

Why this exists

A lot of crop failures and wasted fertilizer come down to guesswork — farmers going with what worked last season, or a soil test result that never gets translated into an actual decision. This project takes standard soil/climate readings and turns them into a direct recommendation, using the same kind of models agronomists already use to study these datasets.
