A note on the numbers

The fertilizer dataset only has 99 rows, so its reported accuracy should be read as "this model recognises the patterns in this small, fairly clean dataset well" rather than "this will always be right in the field." Real fertilizer decisions should also factor in local soil testing and agronomic advice — this tool is meant to point you in a reasonable direction, not replace that.

Why Random Forest

Both datasets are small and tabular with a mix of numeric and categorical features. A Random Forest handles that combination well without much tuning, and — unlike a single Decision Tree — it's less likely to just memorise quirks of the training data.
